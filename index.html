TACTICAL PLAYBOOK

💣 Attacking

1. Identify the weaker bomb site using early information.

2. Use smokes to block long sightlines and flashes to clear close angles.

3. Enter together. The first player clears while the second player trades.

4. Plant only when teammates can protect the site.

5. Spread into crossfires after planting.

🛡️ Defending

1. Establish safe early positions.

2. Use Trophy Systems against incoming explosives.

3. Avoid unnecessary early challenges.

4. Rotate when reliable information confirms pressure on the opposite site.

5. Play the bomb timer and coordinate your retake.

Competitive Mechanics

ADVANCED

⚡ Slide-Canceling

Sprint toward your target, slide into cover, then cancel the slide using your movement controls and layout. Re-center your crosshair before firing. Practice the timing in the training range.

🎯 Dropshotting

Start firing, then activate crouch or prone while maintaining aim. Track the enemy through the movement. Use it selectively so you remain unpredictable.

🏆 Scorestreaks

UAV: Team information.
Counter-UAV: Disrupt enemy information.
Predator Missile: Punish grouped enemies.
Advanced UAV: Strong information advantage.

Choose streaks you can consistently earn.

🎮 HUD & Controls

Use a layout that lets you aim, fire, slide, jump and crouch without losing control of your movement. Change one control at a time and practise before ranked matches.
